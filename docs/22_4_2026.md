## 5. Feasibility: how close FPGA results can be to your simulation results

## 5.1 Functional correctness feasibility
**High feasibility (8/10)**, because your stack is already RTL-first and fixed-point aware.

What can still diverge:
- Timing/handshake side effects after synthesis
- Quantization path edge-cases when routing/pipelining changes
- AXI/memory arbitration behavior on real board

## 5.2 Performance feasibility
Your docs report simulated cycle targets (e.g., baseline/imprinted/optimized snapshots).  
On first FPGA bring-up, practical expectation is:

- **Clock:** often lower than ideal until timing closure (initially ~80-150MHz typical range)
- **Effective cycles:** can grow due to real memory and control overhead
- **Observed throughput:** often **~0.5x to 0.9x** of optimistic pure-sim estimates before full optimization

So yes, **you can get similar trendlines and relative gains**, but absolute token/s should be communicated with a measured hardware envelope, not simulation-only peak.
