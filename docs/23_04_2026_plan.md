## 6) Critical Evaluation Of Your Current Approach

This section is intentionally direct.

## What you are doing very well

1. You built a serious fail-closed validation culture early. That is rare and very valuable.
2. You are treating claims as evidence products, not marketing blurbs. This is exactly correct for technical credibility.
3. You are combining architecture, tooling, and presentation layers instead of building in silos.
4. You are willing to run deep audits before coding, which reduces blind implementation churn.

## Where your approach is currently weak

1. Too many strategic fronts are opened at once.
   - Frontier model expansion, website polish, hardware scaling, agent orchestration, and narrative packaging are all active simultaneously.
   - This increases context-switch cost and slows real closure.
2. You are at risk of measurement ambiguity.
   - Without strict claim-class boundaries, it becomes easy to accidentally mix simulation speedups with software benchmark outcomes.
3. You are still slightly over-indexed on analysis depth vs closure velocity.
   - Deep analysis helped, but now each cycle needs tighter ship criteria.
4. Mobile quality confidence is behind your public-readiness ambitions.
   - This is a practical credibility gap, not a cosmetic issue.

## Most important correction

Move from broad exploration mode to gated execution mode:
1. Limit active work-in-progress to two workstreams at a time.
2. Require explicit exit criteria before opening new fronts.
3. Fail fast on ambiguous metric lineage.
